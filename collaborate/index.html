Give us a component you care about.

You bring a real load envelope, boundary conditions, and a component worth improving. PIE evolves candidates against your constraints and solver-certifies the survivors. You get print-ready geometry plus the full evidence chain.

The loop we want to run with you

  1. Declare

    Your envelope and load cases go into a preregistration with a claim ceiling — you see the strongest possible claim before anything runs.

  2. Evolve + certify

    Search runs under hard physics gates; winners are certified by CalculiX on the as-printed mesh. You get 3MF/STL, per-load-case results, manufacturability audit, and a claims manifest you can verify yourself.

  3. Print + test

    You (or we) print the coupon. CT, cold-flow, thermal, mechanical — your bench data feeds back into the engine's ground-truth ledger.

  4. Repeat

    Each cycle, the engine's models are recalibrated against your physical reality, not a textbook's.

What you will never get from us: a number we can't trace, a proxy result dressed as a certified one, or a "lighter / better / faster" headline that hasn't beaten your baseline on every gate it was declared against.
