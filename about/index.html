About

One engineer. Two AI lanes. Physics as the referee.

PIE (Personal Invention Engine) is a one-engineer operation based in Türkiye.

The engine lane writes and runs the evolutionary machinery. An independent verification lane builds the oracles, grades every claim, and maintains the ledger. The separation is deliberate: it is the same discipline as a design office and an independent stress office — implemented in software, from day one.
